## Frequently asked questions

### How does the home office deduction tool work?
It calculates your office's percentage of the home (office square feet ÷ total square feet) and applies that percentage to your eligible home expenses — mortgage interest, property taxes, utilities, insurance, and repairs — using the regular (actual-expense) method.

### Which mileage rates does the mileage tool use?
It uses the published 2024 IRS standard mileage rates for business, medical, and charitable driving.
