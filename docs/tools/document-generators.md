## How to use a generator

1. Open a generator and fill in the parties, dates, and terms.
2. Press **Generate** to render a formatted document.
3. Review it, then use **Print / Download PDF** to export.
4. Have an attorney review the final document before signing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are these documents legally binding?
The generators produce templates, not legal advice. A template becomes a binding document only when properly completed, executed, and (for some documents like a power of attorney) notarized or witnessed per your state's requirements. Have a qualified attorney review anything important.

### Can I leave optional fields blank?
Yes. Optional fields fall back to sensible default language so the generated document is still complete and readable.
