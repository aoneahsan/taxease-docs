# Personal Finance Calculators

5 of them run inside TaxEase (retirement, budget, debt payoff, credit-card payoff, net worth). **Mortgage, Loan Payment, Auto Loan, Compound Interest, and Savings Goal now open on [ZTools](https://ztools.zaions.com)** — same developer, also free; the links below redirect there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do these account for taxes and inflation?
Most are pre-tax, nominal estimates using the rates you enter. They illustrate the mechanics of loans and savings; for retirement and investment planning, consult a financial advisor.

### Can I compare debt payoff strategies?
Yes. The Debt Payoff calculator lets you compare the snowball (smallest balance first) and avalanche (highest APR first) strategies and shows the projected debt-free date and total interest for each.
