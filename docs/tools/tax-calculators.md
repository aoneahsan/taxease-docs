## Frequently asked questions

### Which tax year do these use?
The calculators use 2024 federal brackets, standard deductions, and FICA rates. Tax law changes yearly, so treat results as estimates and verify current figures before filing.

### Are these calculators only for the United States?
Yes. The tax calculators are based on U.S. federal rules. For other jurisdictions, treat the formulas as general illustrations only.

### Is my income data sent anywhere?
No. Every calculation runs in your browser. Your inputs are not transmitted to a server to produce a result.
